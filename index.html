02 / Media literacy

Deepfake Awareness

Assess context and provenance, not just appearance.

◉

Pause before sharing

AI-generated media can look convincing. No single visual clue proves something is fake.

  • Look for the original source and publication date.
  • Check whether reputable independent sources confirm the claim.
  • Be cautious of urgent requests for money or secrecy.
  • Verify unexpected voice or video requests through another channel.

When something seems wrong

  • Pause. Do not click, forward, pay, or share a verification code.
  • Contact the organisation through its independently verified official channel.
  • Save relevant evidence without spreading suspicious links.
  • In India, report suspected cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • If money has been lost to cyber fraud in India, contact your bank and call 1930 promptly.
This checklist does not remotely monitor devices, block messages, or notify family members. Progress is kept only in the current page session.
